Refund Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. 3-Day Free Trial

Blinders offers a 3-day free trial with full access to all features. During this trial period, you will not be charged. Your subscription will only begin after the 3-day trial period expires.

2. Refund Eligibility

If you request a refund within 14 days of your initial paid subscription (after the free trial ends), we will process a full refund of your payment. Requests must be made within this 14-day window from your first charge date.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at blinders25@gmail.com with:

  • Your account email address
  • The subscription date
  • The reason for the refund request

4. Refund Processing

Once we receive your refund request and verify eligibility, we will process your refund within 5-10 business days. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method.

5. Refunds After 14 Days

Refunds are not available for purchases made more than 14 days after the initial paid subscription date. However, you can cancel your subscription at any time, and you will not be charged for the next billing cycle.

6. Monthly Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your monthly Blinders Pro subscription at any time. Your cancellation will take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, and you will not be charged again. No refund will be issued for the current month's subscription.

7. Exceptions

Refunds may be issued at Blinders' sole discretion for:

  • Duplicate charges due to technical errors
  • Unauthorized transactions (fraud)
  • Service outages or technical failures affecting your ability to use the service

8. Contact Support

For any refund-related questions or to initiate a refund request, please contact us at blinders25@gmail.com.
